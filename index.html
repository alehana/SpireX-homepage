About Us

SpireX started with a simple idea, to give students a place where they can learn real skills and not just theory. We work with beginners and help them grow step by step. Our team believes that everyone can learn to code and build something great if they get the right guidance and enough practice.

Internship Program

Our internship program is made for students who want to gain real experience. You will work on small projects and learn from people who already work in the field.

Web Development

Learn HTML, CSS and JavaScript by building real websites.

Graphic Design

Learn how to design posters, logos and social media content.

Marketing

Learn how businesses grow using social media and online ads.
